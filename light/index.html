01 · About

Full-stack engineer with an owner’s mindset

I’m a software engineer at Softop Solutions, where since June 2022 I’ve helped build and evolve Eduegate ERP — a multi-tenant, cloud-based school management platform that runs admissions, student lifecycle, fees and accounting, HR & payroll, transport, library, canteen and online store, exams, timetables, lesson plans and progress reports, plus the parent portal and the parent and staff mobile apps that sit on top of it.

I work across the whole stack and the whole lifecycle. A typical feature starts as a conversation with a school or a work item in Azure DevOps, and I carry it through SQL schema and entity models, ASP.NET Web API and business logic, the AngularJS or Razor screen, the SSRS report that prints it, and the release to staging and live. In my first quarter I built more than a hundred of the ERP’s master and transaction screens; since then I’ve owned complete modules such as lesson planning, online exams, the smart timetable scheduler and a transfer-certificate workflow.

The platform serves school groups in India, Qatar and the UAE, so I’m used to multi-school tenancy, right-to-left Arabic interfaces, per-client report variants and the live-support rhythm that comes with real users: reproduce, fix, verify, deploy. Beyond code, I run sprint ceremonies when the Scrum Master is out, review pull requests, mentor junior developers and help colleagues unblock on payments, mail and reporting.
